Ecommerce Website · Scope Boundaries & Terms

What the build covers, what sits outside it, and how it is billed.

In scope

  • Shopify store setup and custom theme
  • Design and build of the 153 counted pages plus system pages
  • Search, filters, cart, checkout, COD and customer accounts
  • Inventory, payment, logistics and CRM integrations
  • Back office roles and a daily operating dashboard
  • Import and publishing of the 146 launch SKUs
  • Distributor enquiry form routed to sales
  • Testing, training, launch and 60 days of bug fixes

Out of scope

  • Product page graphics and banners: separate Website Creatives proposal
  • Distributor portal with login: optional add on
  • Pages beyond the counted 153, charged per page
  • New features or development during the 60 day support window
  • Writing product data from scratch; Client supplies it in the template
  • Photography, video and packaging design
  • Marketplace listings, ads and SEO: separate proposals
  • Reviews, loyalty, subscriptions and ERP replacement

Commercial terms

  • 50% at kick off, balance at handover. Fees exclude GST
  • Two review rounds per design stage, one per SKU batch
  • Client feedback within three business days, or the schedule moves
  • Shopify plan, paid apps, domain and gateway charges paid by Client
  • AMC starts after the 60 day support window, billed monthly
  • Every account, the domain and all store data stay in Client's name
  • Changes after sign off are quoted before work starts
