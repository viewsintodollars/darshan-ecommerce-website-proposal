04
Experience rules

Three jobs the storefront has to do well

Easy to find

Discovery by fragrance, not by SKU code

  • Search suggestions
  • Fragrance and format filters
  • Collections by use and occasion
Easy to trust

Enough detail to decide without asking

  • Clear pack size and format
  • Price and availability
  • Usage and safety guidance
Easy to complete

A checkout that survives a weak connection

  • Fast mobile checkout
  • Transparent shipping and totals
  • Visible confirmation and tracking
Most abandoned orders fail on one of these three, not on price.
05
Distinct paths

Two visitors who want opposite things

Consumer purchase

Buy now, in single units

  • Direct product selection from collections and search
  • Payment in a fast checkout, guest or account
  • Delivery, tracking and post purchase service
  • Reorder from order history
Distributor or export enquiry

Qualify first, quote later

  • Company details and territory captured up front
  • Product interest and case quantities, not single units
  • Structured handoff to the sales team, not to checkout
  • No pricing exposed on the public path
Splitting the paths keeps checkout simple and keeps trade enquiries qualified.
